Privacy Policy

App: FitPet  |  Effective date: May 6, 2026

Overview

FitPet is a habit-tracking app that lets you grow a virtual pet by logging healthy habits. This policy explains what data the app handles and how it is used.

Data stored on your device

All core app data — including your habit logs, pet state, and settings — is stored locally on your device only using an embedded database (Isar). This data is not transmitted to the developer or any third party.

  • Habit logs (exercise, sleep, meals, water, rest)
  • Pet progression state (stage, stats, streaks)
  • App settings (reminder time, dark mode preference)
  • Purchase state (Re-Roll Pass, Streak Shield count)

Rewarded ads (AdMob)

FitPet shows rewarded ads via Google AdMob for optional in-app rewards (re-rolling your egg selection and earning Streak Shields). AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier and other data to serve relevant ads. This data is handled by Google under their own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

You can opt out of personalised ads via your Android device settings: Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Crash reporting and analytics (Firebase)

FitPet uses Firebase Crashlytics to capture crash reports and Firebase Analytics to understand how core features are used (for example, which habit types are logged most often and how many pets are evolved).

Crash payloads and analytics events contain only technical data (device model, OS version, app version, feature name). They never contain your name, pet name, habit log content, or any other personally identifiable information. Firebase is operated by Google under their privacy policy linked above.

In-app purchases

Purchases (Re-Roll Pass, Streak Shield) are processed by Google Play Billing. FitPet does not receive or store your payment information. Purchase transaction data is handled by Google under their own terms.

Notification permission

FitPet may request permission to send local notifications for the optional daily habit reminder. Notifications are scheduled entirely on-device. No notification content is routed through external push notification servers.

Data sharing

FitPet does not sell your data or share it with third parties beyond the services described above (AdMob for ads, Firebase for crash reporting and analytics, Google Play Billing for purchases). Each of these is a standard Google platform service.

Data deletion

You can delete all app data at any time directly inside the app:

  1. Open FitPet
  2. Tap the Settings icon (top-right of the home screen)
  3. Scroll to the Account section
  4. Tap Delete All Data
  5. Confirm in the dialog

This permanently removes all habit logs, pet history (including archived pets), streaks, and purchase state from your device. The action cannot be undone. Because no data is stored on external servers, there is nothing further to delete on our end.

Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data from your device.

To request data deletion or if you have questions, contact us at: getfitpet@gmail.com

Children

FitPet is rated for users aged 18 and over. The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.
