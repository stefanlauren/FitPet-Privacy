Privacy Policy

App: FitPet  |  Effective date: May 6, 2026

Overview

FitPet is a habit-tracking app that lets you grow a virtual pet by logging healthy habits. This policy explains what data the app handles and how it is used.

Data stored on your device

Your personal app data is stored locally on your device only using an embedded database (Isar). This data is not transmitted to the developer's servers.

  • Habit logs (exercise, sleep, meals, water, rest)
  • Pet progression state (stage, stats, streaks)
  • App settings (reminder time, dark mode preference)

Third-party services

FitPet uses the following third-party SDKs, each with their own privacy practices:

  • Google AdMob — serves rewarded video ads (used for optional re-roll and streak-shield features). AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad-interaction data to serve ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — automatically captures crash reports to help fix bugs. Reports contain device state and stack traces; they do not include your name, pet name, or habit log content. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Firebase Analytics — records anonymous usage events (e.g. habit logged, pet evolved) to help improve the app. No personally identifiable information is included in these events. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Google Play Billing — processes in-app purchases (Re-Roll Pass, Streak Shield). Purchase transaction data is handled by Google Play. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Notification permission

FitPet may request permission to send local notifications for the optional daily habit reminder. Notifications are scheduled entirely on-device using Android's local alarm system. No notification content is routed through external servers.

Data deletion

You can delete all app data at any time using Settings → Delete All Data, which permanently removes your pet, habits, history, and purchase state and returns the app to its initial state. You can also uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data from your device.

Children

FitPet does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. The app's personal data (habit logs, pet state) is stored locally only and is never transmitted to the developer.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.
