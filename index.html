Privacy Policy

App: FitPet  |  Effective date: April 21, 2026

Overview

FitPet is a habit-tracking app that lets you grow a virtual pet by logging healthy habits. This policy explains what data the app handles and how it is used.

Data collection

FitPet does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. The app has no backend, no analytics SDK, and no third-party data-sharing integrations.

Data stored on your device

All app data — including your habit logs, pet state, and settings — is stored locally on your device only using an embedded database (Isar). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

  • Habit logs (exercise, sleep, meals, water, rest)
  • Pet progression state (stage, stats, streaks)
  • App settings (reminder time, dark mode preference)

Notification permission

FitPet may request permission to send local notifications for the optional daily habit reminder. Notifications are scheduled entirely on-device using Android's local alarm system. No notification content is routed through external push notification servers. Granting or denying this permission has no effect on data collection — none occurs in either case.

Data sharing

No data is shared with third parties. FitPet does not use advertising networks, analytics services, crash-reporting SDKs, or any other service that would receive information about you or your device.

Data deletion

You can delete all app data at any time using the Reset Progress option in the app's Settings screen, or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling removes all locally stored data from your device.

Children

FitPet does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected at all, there are no age-related restrictions on use.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.
